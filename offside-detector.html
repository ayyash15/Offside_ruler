Advanced Law 11 cases
Beyond this demo
Touching or playing the ball

Any contact with the ball by a player in an offside position, however slight, is involvement — regardless of intent.

Interfering with an opponent

Blocking a defender's line of vision, movement, or challenge for the ball can constitute an offense without ever touching it.

Deliberate play by a defender

A deliberate save or deliberate play by a defender can reset the phase — a deflection or ricochet usually does not.

Deflections, saves, rebounds

Real implementations must distinguish a deliberate defensive action from an involuntary deflection, since only the former resets subsequent involvement.

This demo implements one clean passage of play to illustrate the core distinction — it does not attempt to model the complete official Laws of the Game.
